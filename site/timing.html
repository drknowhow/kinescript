Time is a reference.

A layer can start when another one ends: "at": "intro.out+0.2". Make the intro two seconds longer and everything cued after it moves, in a one-line diff. Cycles and missing ids are reported before anything renders.

Four layers and one number. intro lasts 2.5 seconds; the title starts 0.2 s after it ends, the bar 0.4 s after the title comes in, and the closing line when the title leaves. None of the later layers names a time of its own.

Ends and starts
"at": "intro.out+0.2" starts a layer 0.2 s after intro ends, "title.in+0.4" 0.4 s after the title comes in, and "until": "title.out" ends one with it.
Markers
Top-level "markers": {"drop": 12.5} names a time once, and "at": "drop" starts a layer on it, so a beat moves in one place.
Words
"at": "vo:pic" starts a layer on a word of a voice-over, from the word times kine voice writes. See Sound.
Checked first
A cue to an id that does not exist, or a cycle of layers waiting on each other, is reported by kine check before anything renders.
