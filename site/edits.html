Cuts, transitions and captions.

Footage is edited in the same file as the motion graphics: a cut list of trimmed clips, a transition between two clips named in one word, titles and picture-in-picture over them, and captions timed in the scene.

A video edit

A cut list of three trimmed clips with a punch-in, a zoom and a wipe, then a lower third, a callout, a rounded picture-in-picture and captions. The clips are other demos on this site.
