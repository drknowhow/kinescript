One brand, every video.

A brand is a folder: brand.json with palettes, fonts, text styles, chart colours, logos and where they go, motion, an intro and outro, sound defaults and a grade, beside its logo, font and sound files. A scene names it in one line, and the built-ins draw in it.

One scene, the brand's light and dark palettes

A title card, a bar chart, a pie chart, a caption, a lower third, a logo bug and a closing logo. Both videos are in the made-up Tidewater brand, its logo included; the two scene files differ in their "variant" line. kine check knows the brand too: it reports text and chart colours below the contrast they need, a logo drawn too small, and a scene changing a part the brand locks.

The brand editor: kine brand wizard

A page on 127.0.0.1 to see and change a brand: palettes with their contrast, type, logos and placements, intro and outro, sound, a gallery of the built-ins drawn in the brand, and the raw file. Every change is saved at once and checked; the gallery is drawn by the real renderer. kine brand and the kine_brand MCP tool make every change it makes, the same way, so an agent can do all of it too.

Brands live in a library, ~/.kinescript/brands. kine brand use acme (the editor's Use in project) copies one into the project's brand/ folder and records what it copied, so a video keeps the brand it was made with until you run it again. It refuses to replace a copy edited in the project unless --force.

Text styles
A text layer takes "textStyle": "heading" for the brand's font, size, weight, tracking and case; its own keys still win. Built-in titles take the display face and other text the body face.
Intro and outro
"intro": true on a scene plays the brand's sting first and "outro": true its end card last, each rendered as its own segment and joined to the video, with a sonic logo if the brand has one.
Sound and grade
Per role defaults: the voice backend and speaker, a music bed with its gain and fades, the mix's loudness and ducking. A brand grade gives every video the same colour grade.
From a logo
kine brand import acme logos/full.svg proposes light and dark palettes and chart series from a logo's colours, each text and accent moved until it passes the contrast check; --apply writes them.
Locks
"lock": ["palettes", "fonts"] stops a scene's own theme from changing those parts; kine check reports the scene that tries.
Checked writes
Each write checks the brand and refuses a change that breaks it, and --rev refuses it when the brand changed since it was read, so a person in the editor and an agent over MCP do not overwrite each other.

kine brand

The same operations from the command line and, as kine_brand, over MCP.

  • init acmea new brand, from the Kinescript brand or --from another
  • list / showthe library, and one brand's resolved values
  • use acmecopies it into the project's brand/
  • set acme PATH VALUEchanges one value, such as palettes.dark.accent
  • replace acme FILEreplaces brand.json, checked first
  • add-file / remove-filefonts, logos and sound files, their contents checked
  • check acmethe brand's own problems
  • preview acmea sample frame in the brand, as an image
  • import acme LOGOpalettes proposed from a logo
  • wizardthe brand editor above
